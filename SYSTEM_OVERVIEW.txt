EMAIL OUTREACH TOOL — SYSTEM OVERVIEW
======================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
A single-user tool that finds small local businesses on Google Maps,
researches each one, writes a personalized 3-email cold outreach sequence
(intro + 2 follow-ups) using AI the moment a lead qualifies, and — once you
review and confirm a batch — sends those emails automatically from your own
Gmail accounts, spread across US business hours.

It's built for one person (you) pitching AI automation / web development
services to local businesses. It can run entirely on your own machine, or be
deployed to the cloud (Vercel + Supabase) with a private login gate — either
way, it's a single-user tool with no public sign-up.


WHAT IT DOES, STEP BY STEP
---------------------------
1. SCRAPE
   You give it a business type + location + how many leads you want, and
   choose how to cover the area: auto-split (the system figures out how
   many non-overlapping map areas it needs to search to hit your target,
   and digs deeper into any dense pocket), a manual list of specific
   areas, or a single plain search. It pulls matching businesses from
   Google Maps (name, website, phone, address, category), automatically
   skipping anything already in your database and rotating between
   multiple Apify accounts if one runs low or hits a real usage limit.

2. ENRICH
   For each lead with a website, it reads the site (falling back to a
   real headless browser if the site actively blocks simple requests),
   finds a published contact/founder email (checked in the page's
   structured data too, never guessed), grabs a LinkedIn link if present,
   writes an AI summary of the business, and scores how good a fit they
   are for your offer (0-100). It also runs a free email deliverability
   check at this stage (see below).

3. AUTO-DRAFT
   The moment a lead finishes enrichment and qualifies — a working email
   plus a fit score of 50 or higher, not bounced or invalid — the AI
   writes the cold email and both follow-ups automatically, using your
   profile/bio. Nothing manual here; it's already been pushed to your
   connected Google Sheet by the time you look at it.

4. CREATE A SENDING CAMPAIGN
   From the Sending Campaigns tab, you pull however many leads you want
   from the pool of fresh, qualified, never-yet-contacted leads (hottest
   fit score first). The system automatically and evenly splits those
   leads across your connected Gmail accounts — whichever account gets a
   given lead handles all 3 of that lead's emails, so a conversation
   never switches senders mid-thread.

5. REVIEW + CONFIRM
   Before anything is scheduled, you can preview and edit every lead's
   full 3-email sequence and recipient address. Nothing sends until you
   click "Confirm & Schedule."

6. AUTOMATIC SCHEDULING + SENDING
   Once confirmed, sends are automatically spread across business-hours
   weekdays so no single Gmail account ever exceeds its daily sending
   limit, then dispatched one at a time with random pauses between each.

7. FOLLOW UP AUTOMATICALLY
   If a lead doesn't reply, follow-up 2 and follow-up 3 go out
   automatically after a set number of days (also landing within business
   hours) — unless they reply or the email bounces, in which case
   everything for that lead stops.

8. TRACK
   A "Summary" tab shows totals across every campaign: leads scraped,
   emails sent, follow-ups sent, replies received, and how each sending
   account is performing.


HOW WE KEEP IT SAFE / RELIABLE
--------------------------------
Scraped data and mass outreach both carry real risk — bad addresses, sending
the same person twice, looking spammy, burning out your Gmail account, or
missing most of a market because one search only shows ~120 results. Here's
what's built in to guard against each of those:

- COVERS A WHOLE AREA WITHOUT DOUBLE-SCRAPING
  A single Google Maps search only ever returns about 120 results, no
  matter how many businesses actually exist in that area. The system can
  automatically split a big search into a grid of separate, non-overlapping
  map areas (searching each one independently, and zooming further into any
  area that looks unusually dense) to actually cover a whole city, while
  still catching and discarding any business that shows up in more than one
  area.

- ROTATES ACROSS MULTIPLE SCRAPING ACCOUNTS
  If you've added more than one Apify account, the system drains one
  before moving to the next automatically. If an account hits a real usage
  limit mid-scrape, that specific search area is retried on the next
  account instead of the whole scrape failing.

- STOPS DUPLICATE LEADS AT SCRAPE TIME
  Every business from Google Maps has a unique ID. Before adding a new
  lead, we check if that exact business is already in your database —
  if so, it's skipped, not re-added.

- STOPS DUPLICATE EMAILS AT SEND TIME
  Separately from the above, right before any email goes out, the system
  checks: "has this exact email address already been sent to, under a
  different lead?" If yes, it's blocked.

- CHECKS EMAILS ARE REAL BEFORE SENDING
  Every discovered email is checked for correct formatting and a real,
  working mail server behind its domain (free, automatic, no cost). Fake
  or dead-domain emails are excluded from sending entirely.

- WATCHES FOR BOUNCES
  After sending, the system checks your Gmail inbox for delivery-failure
  messages. If an email actually bounces, that lead is flagged and all
  further follow-ups to them are cancelled automatically.

- CHECKS EVERY EMAIL FOR "SPAMMY" WRITING
  Right before sending, each email is scored for spam-trigger words,
  ALL-CAPS, excessive punctuation, and too many links. Emails that score
  too high are either blocked or flagged, based on your settings.

- SEPARATE DAILY LIMITS PER EMAIL STAGE, PER ACCOUNT
  Each Gmail account has its own daily limit for cold emails, a separate
  one for 2nd follow-ups, and a separate one for 3rd follow-ups — so a
  busy day of follow-ups never crowds out that day's new introductions,
  or vice versa.

- ONLY SENDS DURING BUSINESS HOURS
  Automatically-scheduled emails (both first sends and follow-ups) only
  go out on weekdays within a configurable window (9am-5pm Eastern by
  default) — never overnight or on a weekend. An account that would go
  over its daily limit on one day automatically rolls the extra leads to
  the next available business day.

- NEVER SENDS BACK-TO-BACK
  A random pause happens between every single email sent — this isn't a
  bulk blast, it's paced like a real person sending one email at a time.

- KEEPS FOLLOW-UPS FROM THE SAME SENDER
  If email #1 to a lead went out from Account A, follow-ups #2 and #3 to
  that same lead always go from Account A too — decided up front when you
  create the sending campaign, never switched mid-conversation.

- STOPS THE MOMENT SOMEONE REPLIES
  Any real reply on a thread immediately cancels that lead's remaining
  follow-ups.

None of this guarantees a perfect send rate — real inbox-level email
verification (confirming a specific mailbox exists, not just that the
domain has a mail server) would need a paid third-party service, which
isn't connected by default. Everything above is the free, local layer of
protection, and it catches the large majority of real-world risk from
scraped-data outreach.


RUNNING IT: LOCAL VS. DEPLOYED
--------------------------------
Locally, a background process checks for due sends/follow-ups/replies every
so often while "npm run dev" is running — close it and that pauses until you
start it again (nothing is lost, it just catches up).

Deployed to Vercel, there's no always-on process, so an external scheduled
job (hit once a minute, free via a service like cron-job.org) triggers the
same checks instead. Either way, a private login screen (backed by
Supabase, one account only, no public sign-up) sits in front of the whole
app once deployed.
